## Migration plan

Next free number is `061`. `059` and `060` are already written and not applied (`rebrand-ruzz`); hosted has 001 to 058 applied, so Phase 3 would apply 059, 060 and 061 together.

Hosted and local definitions match: for the 14 functions and 2 triggers the md5 of `pg_get_functiondef` / `pg_get_triggerdef` on hosted equals the one on a fully migrated local database. The new definitions are therefore generated from `pg_get_functiondef` with only the matched text replaced.

| Object | Change |
